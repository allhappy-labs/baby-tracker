Growth inside Insights: which section layout?

The separate Growth details screen goes away. Growth sits at the bottom of the Insights scroll (it changes rarely) and ignores Week/Month/All: its charts always run from birth. Shaded band = Swiss PEZZ 3rd–97th percentile, dashed line = median, green = the baby. Head circumference has no reference, so it shows the raw chart only. Editing and deleting a check-in moves to History (filtered to Growth); new check-ins still start from Home → Measure. Tapping a point shows its date, value and percentile.
