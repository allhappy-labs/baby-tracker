Full Insights screen: does this read right?

This is layout A with G1 at the end. There's nothing to pick on this page; tell me what's wrong. The screens are drawn at full length rather than cropped. The header (name + Week/Month/All) stays pinned while you scroll.

  • Averages use complete days only. Today appears as a hatched "so far" bar but never counts in an average or a comparison. Days before tracking began, and days with nothing logged at all, are left out of averages so they don't drag them down.
  • Comparisons are neutral: ▲/▼ against the previous period, in grey, never green or red. A difference smaller than a rounding step shows "≈". "All" has no comparison and shows weekly bars instead.
  • Sections are marked by icon only (no "FEEDING" eyebrow), following the Home/History copy rules. The unit after the big number says what it is.
  • Tap or drag across a chart to see that day's (or week's) exact value in a callout, as shown on the sleep chart.
  • Rows only appear when they apply: no bottle feeds → no bottle rows; no breast feeds → no L/R row.
