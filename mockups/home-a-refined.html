Direction A, refined — Home has two honest states

The dial is gone from the idle screen. With nothing running there is no quantity to plot and no target to plot it against, so Home simply reports the last feed, the last sleep, and the last diaper. The dial returns only when a timer is actually counting, and even then its ring shows seconds of the current minute — real data, not a forecast.

A1 — three cards

Reads best in the dark. Each number is ~40pt, so the three facts are legible without focusing. Nothing in the content area is a control, so picking the phone up to look can't write data.
  • Fills the screen with three facts and nothing else
  • No room for today's totals without scrolling
  • A fourth tracker later means scrolling or shrinking

A2 — three rows + today

Answers a second question too. The totals strip covers "how much has she had today", which otherwise costs a trip to Insights.
  • Relative time drops to ~25pt — still large, less commanding
  • Absorbs a fourth and fifth tracker without redesign
  • Denser; slightly more to parse half-asleep

The running state

One screen, one job. The dock disappears while a timer runs — the actions that matter are switch side and stop, and they take the dock's place so your thumb lands on the same region either way.
  • Stop is a solid filled button: unmissable, one tap
  • Per-side elapsed is visible before you switch, not after
  • The other two trackers stay reported, quietly, below
  • This layout is what the Live Activity mirrors
