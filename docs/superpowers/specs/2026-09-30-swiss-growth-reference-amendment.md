## Intent and release scope

Parents can continue to record weight, length/height, and head circumference in any combination in one dated check-in. Each type gets a trend chart only after two readings of that type. Insights keeps its weight card and **Open growth details** button; new entries start from Home's **Measure** action. Growth details keep edit, delete, history, and per-type charts.

This release uses the sex-specific **Swiss PEZZ 2025** population reference for weight-for-age and length/height-for-age only, from birth through the fifth calendar birthday, subject to verified published table coverage. Head circumference remains recordable and chartable but has no reference percentile or reference band. The Growth UI states that plainly for head readings; it does not present unavailable data as an error or borrow another source. Percentiles are descriptive comparisons, not diagnoses or alerts. The app and dataset must both be distributable without a data-access or license fee.

## Calculation and presentation

Keep calculation on device. The existing profile's required girls/boys reference choice and optional expected due date remain; the server syncs raw measurements and profile fields only. No percentile is stored or exported. The recorded measurement timestamp is interpreted in its stored time zone before age calculation, preserving the existing cross-zone and corrected-age behavior. Use the due date as reference-age origin until the third chronological birthday when it is later than birth; readings before that origin, before birth, or beyond the verified table range show raw values without a percentile. Label a percentile calculated with corrected age.

The paper describes a BCCGo model with median, coefficient of variation, and Box–Cox power. Treat compatibility with the existing LMS arithmetic as a hypothesis until the supplied tables' parameter names, signs, units, and age spacing are verified. Convert exact published parameters into the on-device representation without rounding or extrapolation. Define age conversion and interpolation from the inspected source, then check p3, median, p97, and calculated percentile against independent published values at birth and several ages through five, including both sexes and the height/weight model joins. An unverified, missing, corrupt, or out-of-range row suppresses that calculated insight while the raw reading remains visible.

Keep the entered length/height value and lying/standing method unchanged. The [Swiss pediatric chart](https://files.paediatrieschweiz.ch/production/uploads/2026/07/PCH_Wachstumskurven_260701_DE.pdf) instructs adding **1 cm** when comparing a standing measurement with its 0–2-year length chart. Apply that correction only for a standing reading whose comparison age is before its second calendar birthday, and only to the comparison value. No Swiss source inspected establishes a conversion for a lying reading at or after two years, so show that reading without a percentile. The form preselects lying before the second chronological calendar birthday and standing from that birthday, while allowing parents to record the actual method. Do not reuse WHO's 0.7 cm correction.

The paper's newborn sample contains term births at 39–41 weeks and does not validate a Swiss-specific corrected-age policy. Retain the previously approved corrected-age rule as an explicitly labeled product application of [AAP corrected-age guidance](https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/newborn-infant-and-early-childhood-nutrition/newborn-and-infant-nutrition-assessment-tools/term-infant-growth-tools/), with no claim that the PEZZ study evaluated preterm growth. The app must not use this comparison to diagnose growth concerns.

In Growth details, label available weight/height percentiles and median/3rd–97th bands **Swiss PEZZ 2025 reference**, provide the source and CC BY information, and give an accessible explanation when a value has no reference comparison. Head circumference says **No reference percentile in this release** while preserving its raw chart and history. A reference band appears only when there are at least two eligible observations of that type and never bridges a missing comparison. The Insights card keeps the approved weight-only layout and opens Growth details.
