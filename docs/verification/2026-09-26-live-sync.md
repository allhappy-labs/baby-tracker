## On the phones (risk R1: WebSocket through Tailscale Serve)
Both iPhones got the build via `devicectl`. Server request log after launch, while the caregivers used the app:
- Sockets upgrade through Serve: `GET /v1/live 101`, with lifetimes of 119 s and 129 s. They closed when the app left the foreground, as designed. A socket whose token had expired gets `401`, the phone refreshes, and it redials with a `101`.
- For each write, the writer's `POST /v1/sync` completes, and the other phone's hint-triggered sync starts 60–80 ms later (for example: push done 15:54:58.161, follower sync 15:54:58.219 → done .469). On top of that comes the 300 ms push debounce and one ~250 ms round trip, so a change shows on the other phone about 1 s after the tap. Before this release it took ~17 s (20 s poll).
- No polling traffic while connected; the 300 s safety poll remains.

## Known limits (accepted)
- Live updates need the app in the foreground on the receiving phone. In the background, the Live Activity for a timer started on the other phone still appears only once the app is opened.
- Deferred review minors:
  - A proxy that refuses the upgrade with a 5xx never stops retrying; this is bounded by a 60 s backoff.
  - The "upgrade unsupported" flag lasts for the process lifetime.
  - The close on background is fire-and-forget.
  - The covered-hint check compares the sequence only (costs at most a 300 s poll).
