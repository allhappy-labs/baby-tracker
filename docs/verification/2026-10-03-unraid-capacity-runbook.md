## Window, load and stop conditions

Prepare images/fixture locally before the node window; no compiler runs during measurement. Proposed first approval covers at most 30 minutes including preflight, two sequential pilot cells and cleanup. Each cell is capped at 600 seconds total (including setup/cleanup), with 30-second warmup, 60-second measured arrival window and at most 30-second drain. Pilot is 16 isolated families/32 devices, one writer and one peer per family, 15 writes/minute/writer, 240 planned slots. Preserve offered/attempted/skipped/unfired/acknowledged/peer/error counts and all resource samples. Require at least 200 successful observations for a percentile. Never interpret supervisor completion as passing load.

Do not expand a profile past a failed 32-device pilot. If both fail, end the experiment without an envelope or soak. A later 128/512/shape sweep or 15-minute selected-envelope soak requires its concrete cell list, fixture geometry, total duration and approval; it is not automatically added to this initial 30-minute proposal. HTTP decode timing, native Swift durable-store timing, observable-model timing and rendered/physical acceptance remain distinct. A separate source-attested native Swift proof is necessary for a durable peer claim; a Mac HTTP-only node run supplies none.

Proposed automatic stops, sampled every five seconds: any production readiness failure or container restart/OOM; experiment OOM; any cleanup/ownership error; node available memory below max(2 GiB, 20% physical RAM) for 15 seconds; CPU PSI `some avg10` >20% for 30 seconds; memory PSI `full avg10` >1% for 15 seconds; I/O PSI `full avg10` >10% for 30 seconds; experiment filesystem free space below max(20 GiB, 15%) or free inodes below 10%; production readiness latency above max(1 second, 2x baseline p95) for three consecutive samples. Revalidate numeric thresholds against the fresh baseline before approval; no launch if baseline already violates one, required PSI is absent, or production health cannot be monitored. Thresholds are proposed, not historical measurements or user agreement. Stop issuing load immediately, retain the incomplete receipt, and tear down only owned experiment resources. Do not restart, reconfigure or repair production as part of this experiment.
